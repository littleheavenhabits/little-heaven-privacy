Privacy Policy

App: Little Heaven Habits (Android package: app.littleheaven.habits)
Effective date: November 25, 2026
Short version Little Heaven Habits is a private, offline-first habit tracker. Your habits, notes, schedules, and progress stay on your device. We do not require accounts, do not run ads, do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and do not collect or sell your personal data.

1. Who We Are

Little Heaven Habits is an independent hobby Android application developed and maintained by the Little Heaven Habits developer. If you have any privacy questions or requests, you can contact us at little.heaven.habits@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect and Share

We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal data.

  • The app does not request Android INTERNET permission.
  • There are no user accounts, cloud servers, remote databases, advertising networks, or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs inside the app.
  • All habit names, custom descriptions, check-ins, schedules, preferences, and world decorations are stored exclusively on your device in private app storage (local SQLite/Room database and Android DataStore).

3. Backups and User-Controlled Exports

  • Manual Export & Restore: You may optionally export a backup file of your habits and settings to a location you choose on your device or personal storage provider using Android's file picker. You control where that file is stored and who can access it.
  • Android System Backup: Depending on your Android device settings, Android's built-in backup service (Google Auto Backup) may back up local app data to your personal Google account. You can manage or disable this anytime in your device's Android Backup settings.

4. Permissions Used by the App

Little Heaven Habits requests only the minimum permissions needed for optional local features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional; used only if you turn on local habit reminders.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to reschedule your local reminders after your phone restarts.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library without your explicit one-time selection.

5. Voluntary Feedback & Bug Reports

If you choose to tap Suggestions & Bug Report inside Settings, the app prepares a draft email in your own email app addressed to little.heaven.habits@gmail.com. You can review, edit, blur/redact any optional screenshot, and decide whether to send it.

If you choose to email us, we receive only the email address, message, basic app/device version details, and any attachment you voluntarily send. We use this information solely to respond to your message and improve the app, never share it with third parties for marketing, and will delete your correspondence upon request.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your habit data lives only on your device, you have full control over it at all times:

  • You can edit or delete individual habits inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android Settings permanently deletes all local app data on your device.
  • If you previously emailed us feedback and would like your email deleted, contact little.heaven.habits@gmail.com.

7. Target Audience and Children's Privacy

Little Heaven Habits is designed for a general audience of teenagers and adults and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Because the app operates offline without accounts, advertising, or remote data collection, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future versions of Little Heaven Habits introduce optional online features, we will update this Privacy Policy and inform users before any new data processing begins. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.

9. Contact
